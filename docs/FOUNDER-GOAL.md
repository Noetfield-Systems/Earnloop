# EARNLOOP — FOUNDER GOAL (locked)
Status: Locked for product prioritization; changed only by explicit Founder decision.

The product exists to replicate proven profitable-mechanics businesses with near-zero initial capital: Freecash, Reward XP, LootUp, Mistplay, Zealy, Fluz and their commercial suppliers. Be revenue-first and seek independently verifiable, advertiser-funded customer acquisition and a genuinely positive contribution margin. Community, identity, quests, loyalty, rewards, referral and eventually gift-card redemption are *distribution and retention mechanics*, not the primary objective.

## Outcome ordering
1. First real paying advertiser / approved monetization supply and contract.
2. First externally verified payable advertiser conversion, positive transaction contribution.
3. Reward actually funded and delivered after appropriate member verification and settlement.
4. Repeat positive-margin transactions, organic retained users, contribution profit, then scalable growth.

## Not acceptable
- Claim fictitious offers, paid users, partner approval, or cash profit.
- Treat community XP as a cash claim or a financial investment.
- Pay members from recruitment fees or promise guaranteed yield.
- Duplicate Sina Founder OS, Noetfield governance SSOT, company registry or generic schedulers.
- Suppress the actual cost of payouts, fraud, processing, support, cloud hosting and partner reversals.

## Company separation
Earnloop is its own product and operation, owned by Noetfield Systems Inc. Its code, release process, market work and product-specific Company OS live in `Noetfield-Systems/earnloop-product` once the separate remote repository is created. Sina Founder OS receives only strategic mission summaries, exceptions requiring founder approval and independently verifiable performance receipts. No shared application database or hidden schedule coupling.
