# EARNLOOP — FOUNDER GOAL (locked)
Status: Locked for product prioritization; changed only by explicit Founder decision.

The product exists to replicate proven profitable-mechanics businesses with near-zero initial capital: Freecash, Reward XP, LootUp, Mistplay, Zealy, Fluz and their commercial suppliers. Be revenue-first and seek independently verifiable, advertiser-funded customer acquisition and a genuinely positive contribution margin. Community, identity, quests, loyalty, rewards, referral and eventually gift-card redemption are *distribution and retention mechanics*, not the primary objective.

## Outcome ordering
1. First real paying advertiser / approved monetization supply and contract.
2. First externally verified payable advertiser conversion, positive transaction contribution.
3. Reward actually funded and delivered after appropriate member verification and settlement.
4. Repeat positive-margin transactions, organic retained users, contribution profit, then scalable growth.

## Not acceptable
- Claim fictitious offers, paid users, partner approval, or cash profit.
- Treat community XP as a cash claim or a financial investment.
- Pay members from recruitment fees or promise guaranteed yield.
- Duplicate Sina Founder OS, Noetfield governance SSOT, company registry or generic schedulers.
- Suppress the actual cost of payouts, fraud, processing, support, cloud hosting and partner reversals.

## Company separation
Earnloop is an **independent Founder project and operation**, not a Noetfield Systems Inc. subsidiary or corporate CRM asset. Noetfield may provide its software development and technical execution services only; development, GitHub hosting or an old company/runtime identifier conveys no equity, management, customer, payments, revenue or treasury rights. Earnloop controls its own brand, users, economic model, CRM, outreach and partner accounts. Its source and product-specific execution remain in the canonical [`Noetfield-Systems/Earnloop`](https://github.com/Noetfield-Systems/Earnloop) repository, with production on Railway; do not revive the old app path in Sina Founder OS. Sina Founder OS receives only authorized scoped mission summaries and verified performance receipts. No shared application database, contacts, consent or hidden schedule coupling.

## Revenue proof boundary (implementation status, 2026-11-09)
The product has a publisher-gated Torox adapter and a local Company OS execution loop; no real publisher contract, revenue settlement, funded reward delivery or independently verified positive margin is established by that implementation. Partner postbacks and rows with status `settled` are NOT proof of money collected. Company OS commercial prioritization must remain on external reconciliation until a bank/provider/fulfillment evidence-backed ledger exists. Organic signup and noncash community growth can proceed in parallel without fabricated rewards. Reference: `docs/EXECUTION-ORDER-v1.md` and the single Founder Goal in Sina Founder OS.
