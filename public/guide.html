Know your rewards.

Start with community XP

Daily check-ins earn 15 XP. Our daily number puzzle earns 10 XP for a correct answer within three attempts. Seven distinct check-in days unlock a one-time 70 XP bonus. XP measures participation; it cannot be sold, withdrawn or redeemed for gift cards.

What a partner report means

An advertiser may report an action before approving payment. Reports can be reversed or disputed. Reporting, approval, cash collection, reward funding and delivery are separate steps. Earnloop does not currently offer cash redemption.

Check each offer before starting

When approved inventory is available, read the provider’s country, device, new-user, time-limit and completion requirements. Keep the offer reference if you need support. A promise on another website is not an Earnloop reward.

Invites are introductions

Your invite link attributes new member signups. Inviting someone does not earn cash. Any future commercial referral benefit needs explicit terms and a real funding source.

Keep your session safe

Your current membership belongs to this browser session. Clearing cookies can remove access; cross-device account recovery is not available. Do not share cookies or session information.
