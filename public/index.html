GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Everything you
need to know.

Is Earnloop free to join?

Yes. Creating a founding explorer session and claiming community XP is free. You do not need a credit card or make a purchase.

Can I turn community XP into cash or gift cards?

No. Founding XP is a community progress score, not money and not redeemable. Future sponsored rewards will be offered only after approved partner funding and a verified fulfillment system are in place.

How do referrals work?

Each explorer has a unique invitation code. When a new visitor joins using your link, the invite is tracked. Invites are not cash credits, and no referral payout is promised.

How are offers and conversions verified?

Sponsored offers are unavailable until a provider is approved and configured. Provider callbacks will be recorded separately from cash received, with reversal and settlement checks before withdrawals can exist.

How does my daily streak work?

Claim one daily check-in per UTC day. A check-in the next UTC day continues your streak; skipping a day starts a new streak. The server validates each claim.
