REWARDS TRANSPARENCY · GUIDE

Why your offerwall reward might still be pending.

Finishing a game milestone on your device, receiving a tracking callback, and getting paid are different events. This guide explains what evidence matters, why delays occur, and how to ask for help.

1. Starting a trackable offer

Each campaign has eligibility rules. Use the correct invitation or offer link on the device you intend to use, avoid switching browsers mid-process, and record the provider name, campaign title and start date. Keep only information you are comfortable sharing in a support request; never send a password or payment-card details.

2. Reported conversion is not approved payment

A provider callback may report that a milestone took place. Reporting does not by itself prove the action qualified, that the advertiser accepted the action, or that the provider has paid the publisher. Some providers allow postback reversals when a conversion is invalidated. Earnloop stores source reports separately from verified reward obligations.

3. Common reasons for a missing or delayed credit

  • The advertiser has not yet reported the qualifying milestone or has a review period.
  • The campaign was not available for the country, operating system or account's first-time-user status.
  • The offer was opened or completed through an untracked path.
  • A stated deadline, milestone or other campaign requirement was not satisfied.
  • The provider reported an adjustment or reversal. An independently checked dispute may still be possible.

A missing callback is not automatically evidence of fraud by either party. Preserve evidence and use the documented support process.

4. When can a reward be redeemed?

Only an explicitly approved reward supported by funded, usable payout capacity can enter a payable wallet. Rewards can then be reserved, submitted to a payout partner, processed and ultimately fulfilled. A successful API request does not prove gift-card delivery. Some payout methods have geographic and denomination restrictions.

5. How to open an Earnloop missing-credit case

If you are an Earnloop member, open your Earn activity page. Under “Your offer activity,” review source conversion and reversal reports. Use “Missing credit or offer problem?” with the campaign name, approximate date, milestone and source event ID when available. Your case stays linked to your member session. The team must still confirm eligibility with the actual advertising provider.

6. Current Earnloop availability

Earnloop's founding beta currently offers community participation and non-cash XP. Real paid inventory and cash redemption require approved advertiser terms and verified funding; they should not be assumed active because a technical integration exists. No fixed earnings per hour, guaranteed cash-out date or successful payout count is claimed.
